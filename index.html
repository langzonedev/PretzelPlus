Good to know

Before you enquire.

These answers describe the current direction and remain placeholders until operating details are finalised.

What kinds of events are a good fit?

Pretzel Plus is being shaped for suitable markets, festivals, community gatherings, public events and selected private occasions. Enquire with your details so the fit can be assessed.

Where will Pretzel Plus operate?

The initial direction is event-based trading in South Australia. Confirmed service areas and upcoming appearances will be published as plans develop.

Can you serve a large crowd?

Capacity will depend on attendance, trading hours, site access, utilities and the final menu. These details are reviewed before a booking is confirmed.

Will dietary options be available?

Ingredients, allergens and dietary suitability will be published only after recipes, suppliers and preparation processes are confirmed.
